Point the bottom edge of the phone (speaker and mic) at what you want to measure. Capture a background with nothing moving; after that the cyan trace shows only what moves, like a person walking past.

Hold the phone flat at chest height, bottom edge pointing outward, and turn slowly on the spot (about 10 seconds per full circle). Each echo is drawn at its distance in the direction you were facing. Up is the direction you faced when the map was cleared.

Measure accuracy with a tape measure. Stand facing a flat wall with nothing in between, point the bottom edge at it, enter the true distance from the phone's bottom edge, and press Measure. Try 0.5, 1, 2 and 3 m.
