Press Start scan, then turn slowly on the spot

Hold the phone upright in portrait and turn slowly in a full circle, then tilt up and down to fill in the floor and ceiling. Each time you face a new direction the camera frame is turned into depth and added to the cloud. Drag the cloud to look around it. The first start downloads the depth model (about 25–50 MB).

Settings

A single camera can't measure true size, so distances are approximate: the scan is scaled so the middle of the first view sits at the distance above. Shapes come from an AI depth model (Depth Anything V2), not from a laser.

Sonar mode measures real distances with sound.
